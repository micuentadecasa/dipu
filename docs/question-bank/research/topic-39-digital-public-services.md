# Topic 39 — Digital public services and official electronic channels

**Research date:** 2026-10-13  
**Evidence policy:** only current official primary sources were used. Claims about implementation-specific shared services were not inferred from the syllabus or from blocked portal pages.

## Evidence boundary

The official call names identification/signature, citizen folder and records, intermediation, contracting, subsidies, and electronic invoicing. The SGAD CTT pages for SCSP, Red SARA, SIR, PID, FIRe and Cl@ve remained behind the official WAF `Request Rejected` response during probing. Therefore this topic uses the independently reachable official Cl@ve public pages for generic Cl@ve behaviour, the consolidated BOE text of Royal Decree 203/2021 for the legal electronic-administration framework, the official Facturae portal and Law 25/2013 for electronic invoicing, and consolidated Laws 9/2017 and 38/2003 for contracting and subsidies. No question asserts an unsupported CTT service endpoint, implementation detail, or local platform workflow.

## Official sources

- `BOE-RD203-2021`: Royal Decree 203/2021, consolidated text. Articles 5–61 and the preamble cover portals, PAGe, Carpeta Ciudadana, electronic seats, automated action, identification, signatures, CSV, registers, notifications, archives and validation platforms. The current consolidated text includes the 2025 amendment to article 27.1. Retrieved and verified 2026-10-13.
- `CLAVE-OFFICIAL`: Government of Spain Cl@ve pages: “¿Qué es Cl@ve?”, “¿Cómo funciona?” and “¿Qué es la firma centralizada?”. Retrieved and verified 2026-10-13.
- `FACTURAE-OFFICIAL`: Government of Spain Facturae portal, “¿Qué es la factura electrónica?” and FACe entry-point information. Retrieved and verified 2026-10-13.
- `BOE-L25-2013`: Law 25/2013, consolidated text. Articles 1, 4–12 and additional provision 3 cover public-sector electronic invoices, entry points and accounting registers. Retrieved and verified 2026-10-13.
- `BOE-LCSP-2017`: Law 9/2017, consolidated text. Articles 1, 28, 63, 99 and 118 cover public-contracting principles, need, contractor profile, lots and minor contracts. Retrieved and verified 2026-10-13.
- `BOE-LGS-2003`: Law 38/2003, consolidated text. Articles 2, 8–14, 20, 22–23, 34 and 41 cover subsidies, beneficiaries, BDNS, concession and reimbursement. Retrieved and verified 2026-10-13.
