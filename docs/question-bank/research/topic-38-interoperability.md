# Topic 38 — Interoperabilidad estatal, SCSP y Red SARA

- **Research date:** 2026-10-13
- **Authoritative sources:** `BOE-RD4-2010` and `BOE-L40-2015`; `BOE-LPACAP-2015` for article 28 document-intermediation rules.
- **Retrieval and verification:** BOE consolidated texts retrieved and checked on 2026-10-13.
- **Evidence boundary:** The PAE service pages for SCSP and Red SARA were attempted but returned the official WAF `Request Rejected` response. They remain blocked in `source-register.json`; no accepted question relies on a PAE-specific implementation claim or on an unverified expansion of the SCSP acronym.

## Verified provisions

- Real Decreto 4/2010, articles 4–7: ENI principles, integral and multidimensional interoperability, and multilateral solutions.
- Real Decreto 4/2010, articles 8–13: interoperable services, inventories, semantic assets, open standards, common infrastructures and Red SARA.
- Real Decreto 4/2010, articles 14–18: addressing, official time, reuse directories, licensing and signature-policy interoperability.
- Real Decreto 4/2010, articles 20–23: validation platforms, preservation, security and formats.
- Real Decreto 4/2010, articles 24–29 and its additional and transitional provisions: digitalisation, conformity controls, technical interoperability standards, common instruments, training and adaptation.
- Real Decreto 4/2010 glossary: open standards, document and metadata concepts, interoperability dimensions, repositories and related terms.
- Ley 40/2015, articles 155–158: transmissions of data, compatible further processing, ENI/ENS and reuse of public-sector applications.
- Ley 39/2015, article 28: electronic recourse to documents already held by an Administration, including platforms of intermediation of data.

The 200 accepted specifications are limited to these provisions and the cited Ley 40/2015 provisions, cite the exact article, paragraph or glossary entry, and were independently keyed before acceptance. PAE-specific SCSP and Red SARA implementation claims remain blocked.
